FloatQueue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

The short version: FloatQueue collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your tasks, categories, schedule windows and history are stored locally on your device (and in the app's own shared container so its widgets can display them). Nothing you enter is uploaded. FloatQueue has no server, no accounts and no advertising. The only network connections the app ever makes are the ones you turn on: Apple's iCloud for Crew, your synced Reminders list, and — only if you opt in — the anonymous usage signals described below.

Anonymous usage signals (optional, off unless you opt in)

During setup (and any time under Rhythm → Usage) you can choose to share anonymous usage signals — which screens are used and where people stop, e.g. "onboarding completed", "paywall viewed". These are sent to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service hosted in the EU. Signals never contain task titles, notes, categories or anything you typed; the installation identifier is hashed before it leaves your device and salted again on receipt, IP addresses are not stored, and nothing can be traced back to you. When you opt in, the small usage log already stored on your device (up to the last 30 days) is included once, so the setup you just completed can be counted. Switch it off any time in the app; nothing already sent can identify you. If you never opt in, no signal is ever sent. The app also keeps a small usage log on your device so you can see the same numbers yourself.

Purchases

FloatQueue Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, and your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to an account with us.

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications are used to remind you about scheduled tasks. Alarms (AlarmKit) ring when a task's time is up, if you enable that per category. Both are scheduled and handled entirely on your device.

iCloud and backups

FloatQueue does not sync your data itself. Your device's standard backup (iCloud or computer backup) may include the app's data, under Apple's own terms — that is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Crew (optional sharing)

If you use Crew — inviting someone to float tasks into your queue — those items travel through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, end to end. The developer operates no server and can never see, store, or access anything you or your crew send. An invite link grants exactly one person the ability to send items to you; you can pause or revoke it at any time in the app. If you never use Crew, nothing leaves your device at all.

Reminders bridge (optional)

If you designate an Apple Reminders list to sync, the app reads and writes that one list only: reminders in it become tasks in your queue, and completing, editing or deleting on either side is mirrored to the other. This uses Apple's EventKit entirely on-device; reminder contents are never sent anywhere. Turn the bridge off at any time by setting the list to Off, and revoke access entirely in iOS Settings.

TestFlight beta

If you use FloatQueue through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs, usage statistics and any feedback or screenshots you choose to submit, and shares them with the developer to improve the app. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms; the app itself still collects nothing.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. There is nothing to request or erase anywhere else: nothing personal ever leaves your device, and any anonymous signals you chose to send cannot be linked to you.
